Privacy policy.

EFFECTIVE DATE · 28 SEPTEMBER 2026

Legion Builder ("the app") is an offline army list builder for tabletop miniatures games, published by Krzysztof Jablonski.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

The armies you create are saved in a local database on your device. They never leave the device through the app, and the developer has no access to them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Device backups

If Android backup is enabled in your device settings, Android may include the app's local data in your personal Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings and is not accessible to the developer.

Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
